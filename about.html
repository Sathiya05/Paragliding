Our Origin

A Dream That
Took Flight
In 2009.

AeroGlide began in a tiny garage in Interlaken, Switzerland, with a single wing, two pilot friends, and an unshakeable belief: everyone deserves to feel free in the sky.

Founders Marco Bauer and Lena Richter — both world-competition paragliding veterans — turned their passion into a meticulous operation that valued safety above everything. Their first season: 47 tandem flights. Their tenth season: over 2,000.

Today, AeroGlide operates across three Alpine nations, offers both paragliding and hang gliding tandem experiences, and has become the most awarded tandem operation in the region. But the heart of the company remains the same — a single moment of pure, unbridled freedom for every passenger who steps off the mountain with us.

Our Journey

Fifteen Years
Of Milestones

2009

The First Flight

Marco and Lena launch AeroGlide from a rented hangar in Interlaken. First season ends with 47 tandem flights and a 5-star average.

2012

Hang Gliding Added

We introduced tandem hang gliding — becoming one of only three operators in Europe offering both disciplines under one certified brand.

2015

Dolomites Location Opens

Our second launch site opens in northern Italy's breathtaking Dolomite region, tripling our seasonal capacity.

2018

10,000 Flights Milestone

We celebrated our ten-thousandth tandem flight — still with a perfect safety record. Champagne at 3,000 metres.

2021

Pyrenees Base Launched

Our third and most dramatic location opens straddling the French-Spanish border — 2,800m of pure vertical freedom.

Today · 2025

The Sky Is The Limit

12,000+ flights later, we're still growing — new pilots joining, new experiences launching, and the same founding promise: unforgettable, safe, extraordinary flight for everyone.
