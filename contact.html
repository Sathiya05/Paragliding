Pre-Flight Questions

Common questions before you take the leap.

Do I need any prior experience?

No experience is required! For tandem flights, you'll be securely attached to a master pilot who handles everything. You just need to be able to take a few steps during launch.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety is our priority. If wind or visibility conditions are unsafe, we will reschedule your flight for free or provide a full refund of your deposit.

Can I bring my own GoPro?

Yes, as long as it is secured with a secondary tether/leash. We also offer a premium 4K media package where the pilot captures your entire flight from multiple angles.
